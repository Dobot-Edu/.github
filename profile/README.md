
# DOBOT Education

DOBOT Education is dedicated to robotics education, research, and open-source development, providing developers, researchers, teachers, and students with robotics platforms, development tools, and application examples.

Our projects cover embodied AI, robot manipulation and teleoperation, humanoid and quadruped applications, simulation and training, and robot SDKs.

🌐 **Website:** [DOBOT Official Website](https://www.dobot-robots.com/)  
📦 **Repositories:** [Dobot-Edu on GitHub](https://github.com/Dobot-Edu?tab=repositories)

## About DOBOT

DOBOT develops intelligent robotic solutions for education, research, industry, and embodied intelligence.

Through the Dobot-Edu open-source community, we aim to make robotics development more accessible by providing reusable projects, application examples, development frameworks, and educational resources.

For more information, visit the [DOBOT Official Website](https://www.dobot-robots.com/).
